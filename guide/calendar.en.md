# Calendar

See your tasks on a real calendar — day, week, and month views, right inside
Todoister.

## Open
- In the sidebar, click **Calendar** (the green button below the main list).
- Use the switch on the right to pick **Day**, **Week**, or **Month** — your
  choice is remembered.
- **Today** jumps back to the current date; the **‹ ›** arrows move by day, week,
  or month, depending on the view.

## What you see
- Only tasks **with a date** appear; completed ones don't.
- A **timed** task sits on the hour grid; the colored bar on its left is its
  priority.
- A dated but **untimed** task sits at the top, in the **All-day** row.
- A red line marks the current time; in Month view **"+N more"** opens that day.

## Adding
- Click an **empty spot** on the grid to open Quick Add — the date and time are
  already filled in.
- Clicking an existing task opens it, as everywhere.

## Completing
- Hover a task → a **circle** appears; click it to complete the task and remove
  it from the grid.
- Did it by accident? The **Undo** button in the bottom toast brings it back.

## Connecting Google Calendar
Viewing your Google calendar here, or sending tasks to it, lives on its own
page — see **"Calendar — simple connection"** and
**"Calendar — full sync"** in Help.
