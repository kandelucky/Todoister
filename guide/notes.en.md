# Notes

The **Notebook** is a real note-taking space living next to your tasks.

## Turning notes on and off
- Notes are **optional**. If you've never used them, the sidebar shows an
  **"Enable notes"** row at the top of *My Projects* — click it to turn them on.
- Notes live in a real project called **Notebook**, so they use **one of your 5
  free project slots**. If all 5 are taken, free one up first.
- To turn notes off, open the Notebook's **⋯** menu:
  - **Archive** — hides notes but keeps them. "Enable notes" brings them back.
  - **Delete** — removes the Notebook and **all notes permanently**.

## Across your devices
Because Notebook is a real synced project, your notes travel with your account.
Sign in from another computer or your phone and the **Notebook** appears
automatically with all its pages — nothing to set up.

## Using it
- Open the **Notebook** from the sidebar (pinned at the top, with its own icon).
- At the top of the Notebook list there are two small buttons: **new page** (file icon)
  and **new section** (folder icon).
- Each note is a page in a rich editor — headings, lists, tables, images. Type "/" for
  the block menu, and you can paste Markdown with **Ctrl+V**.
- **Sections** — group pages; each section header has a **"+"** to add a page and a
  **"⋯"** menu (rename, reorder, sort, colour, delete). The page's own **⋯** menu can also
  create a brand-new section and move the page into it. Pages with no section sit in a
  faint band above the sections.
- **Attach files** with the paperclip in the bottom bar (up to 5MB). They appear in a
  small **Files** strip above the bottom bar — shown only when the page actually has
  files. (To put an image *inside* the text, use the editor's "/" image block instead.)
- **Bottom bar** on a page: date & **time**, priority colour, labels, attach file · pin,
  move to a section, duplicate, archive, **export** · delete.
- **Export** a page to your browser (for printing / Save as PDF) or to a Markdown file.
- **Pin** important notes or **archive** old ones.

The pinned and archived groups follow your current language and are matched by
their emoji, so switching language never renames or duplicates them.
