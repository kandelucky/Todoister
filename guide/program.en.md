# Program

Todoister is your personal task hub. It keeps your Todoist data locally and
gives you everything in a fast, native Windows app. No browser needed.
Everything you use in Todoist is here — plus our additions.

## Getting started

- Todoister installs in a snap and launches from the desktop shortcut or the
  Start menu.

## First launch

- On the first run a welcome window walks you through step by step.
- Syncing needs a Todoist API token. See the detailed instruction on the
  **"Sync"** help page.
- The first sync downloads your projects, sections and tasks.

## Interface

- **Sidebar** — the classic Todoist sidebar, joined by the Notebook (if you
  want it), Google Calendar and the Buddy (planned).
- **Top bar** — standard Todoist, plus new icons, information and sticky
  notes.
- **Main area** — list, board (columns by section), calendar or notes
  (Notebook).

## Tasks

- Add, edit and make quick changes right from the list or the detail window.
- Supported: description, priority, date, time, labels, subtasks and
  comments.
- Drag between sections and reorder.
- Search across tasks, descriptions, labels and sections.
- Undo / Redo.
- Priorities (P1–P4) — give a task its colour (red, orange, blue, plain) and
  mark sticky notes with the priority colour too; priority-driven reminder
  automation in the calendar is planned.

## What makes Todoister unique

Todoister keeps Todoist's simplicity and power, but adds features that
ordinary task managers don't have:

- **Notebook** — a full note-taking space next to your tasks. Create pages,
  add text, images and files, organise them into sections and keep your
  ideas in one place.
- **Sticky Notes** — your most important tasks always in sight. Pin priority
  items and they stay on screen until they're done.
- **Calendar** — manage tasks in day, week and month views. Quick sync with
  Google Calendar, or full integration where tasks work as real calendar
  events.
- **Buddy (planned)** — a little digital companion that grows with your
  progress and turns finished work into a more motivating experience.

Each feature has its own help page in this same menu.
