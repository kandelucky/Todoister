# Program

Todoister is your personal task hub. It keeps your Todoist data locally and
gives you everything in a fast, native Windows app. No browser needed.
Everything you use in Todoist is here — plus our additions.

## Getting started

- Todoister installs in a snap and launches from the desktop shortcut or the
  Start menu.
- Only one copy runs at a time: launching the shortcut again simply brings the
  existing window to the front.

## Background mode and the system tray

- The window's **X** does not quit the app — it just hides it, and Todoister
  stays in the **system tray** (next to the clock). Sync keeps running in the
  background.
- **Tray icon** — a red dot appears when you have tasks due today or overdue;
  the exact number is on the app's taskbar icon badge and in the tray icon's
  tooltip.
- **Right-click the tray icon** — the menu:
  - Open (with the today/overdue count in brackets)
  - your pinned sticky notes — the first two directly, the rest behind a
    "+N" row; each can be opened or completed without showing the window
  - Add task · Today · Inbox · Sync now
  - Start with Windows (on/off)
  - Quit — the only place that really closes the app
- If you can't see the tray icon, it may be in the hidden icons (^) — drag it
  out next to the clock so it is always visible.

## First launch

- On the first run a welcome window walks you through step by step.
- Syncing needs a Todoist API token. See the detailed instruction on the
  **"Sync"** help page.
- The first sync downloads your projects, sections and tasks.

## Interface

- **Sidebar** — the classic Todoist sidebar, joined by the Notebook (if you
  want it), Google Calendar and the Buddy (planned).
- **Top bar** — standard Todoist, plus new icons, information and sticky
  notes.
- **Main area** — list, board (columns by section), calendar or notes
  (Notebook).

## Tasks

- Add, edit and make quick changes right from the list or the detail window.
- Supported: description, priority, date, time, labels, subtasks and
  comments.
- Drag between sections and reorder.
- Search across tasks, descriptions, labels and sections.
- Undo / Redo.
- Priorities (P1–P4) — give a task its colour (red, orange, blue, plain) and
  mark sticky notes with the priority colour too; priority-driven reminder
  automation in the calendar is planned.

## What makes Todoister unique

Todoister keeps Todoist's simplicity and power, but adds features that
ordinary task managers don't have:

- **Notebook** — a full note-taking space next to your tasks. Create pages,
  add text, images and files, organise them into sections and keep your
  ideas in one place.
- **Sticky Notes** — your most important tasks always in sight. Pin priority
  items and they stay on screen until they're done. Details below.
- **Calendar** — manage tasks in day, week and month views. Quick sync with
  Google Calendar, or full integration where tasks work as real calendar
  events.
- **Buddy (planned)** — a little digital companion that grows with your
  progress and turns finished work into a more motivating experience.

Each feature has its own help page in this same menu.

## Sticky notes

- **Pin** — the first item in a task's menu (⋯ or right-click), "Add sticky
  note". A pinned task shows a green pin next to ⋯ on its card.
- **Unpin** — from the same menu, by clicking the card's pin, or with the
  sticky note's ×.
- Up to **10** tasks can be pinned at once. **Two** are shown in the top bar;
  the rest wait in a queue — a "+N" card sits next to them.
- Click "+N" to see the **whole queue**: each row has a complete circle, the
  text (opens the task), the due date and × to unpin. The first two rows are
  tinted — those are the ones on screen right now.
- **Order** — soonest due date first; on the same date the higher priority;
  then alphabetical. Tasks without a date go last. When you complete or unpin
  one, the next in the queue surfaces automatically.
- The sticky note's circle takes the priority colour (P1 red, P2 orange,
  P3 blue).
- The same two pinned tasks and the "+N" row appear in the system-tray menu —
  complete or open them without showing the window.
