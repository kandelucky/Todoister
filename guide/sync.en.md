# Sync

Todoister keeps a local copy of your Todoist, so it's always fast — even
offline.

## Your account
- It connects to Todoist with an **API token** (a key). You entered it once, at
  first launch; it's stored only on this computer.
- Click your **name** in the sidebar → a panel shows the status plus
  **"Sync now"** and **"Disconnect"**.
- **"Sync now"** syncs with Todoist immediately; if calendar **full sync** is on,
  it also pushes your tasks to Google Calendar right away.
- To switch accounts: **"Disconnect"** → **"Connect Todoist"** → enter a new
  token.

## How it works
- Every edit is saved **locally and instantly** — no waiting on the network.
- In the background it syncs with Todoist **every 30 seconds**, pushing your
  changes and pulling anything from your phone or the web.

## Offline
- If there's no connection, an **"offline"** pill appears; changes are queued and
  sent automatically once you're back.
- Click the pill to retry now.
